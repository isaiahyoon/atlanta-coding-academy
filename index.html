ATLANTA / FAITH / TECHNOLOGY

Code with purpose.
Build for good.

We envision faith-centered coding education in Atlanta that equips learners with practical skills and helps them create work that serves God and their neighbors.

Connect with Isaiah

01 / THE CALLING

PRACTICAL SKILLS. LASTING PURPOSE.

Technology is a tool.
Service is the point.

Our vision is to widen access to practical technology education and help people use their gifts in service to God and their neighbors.

We hope to welcome every learner with dignity, including people who have been incarcerated, and support them as they turn their ideas into useful, beautiful work.

GRACE / RESTORATION / SERVICE

Beyond the classroom, we hope to contribute to an Atlanta where families can put down roots and flourish.

SCRIPTURE / SPIRIT / WITNESS

Read the Word.
Walk in the Spirit.

We envision Bible study rooted in the complete King James Version, giving learners space to read Scripture for themselves.

John 15:26 reminds us that the Comforter—the Spirit of truth—testifies of Christ.

02 / LEARNING BY BUILDING

FUNDAMENTALS FIRST. GROWTH FOR LIFE.

A path from curiosity
to capability.

Our learning vision brings together in-person coding, Bible study, and creative projects shaped by learners' interests and the communities they hope to serve.

01

Code with craft

Begin with programming and web fundamentals, then explore frontend and backend development, databases, APIs, security, testing, debugging, performance, and deployment.

02

Work with people

Collaborate with engineers, designers, product managers, and peers while developing the judgment to contribute thoughtfully and work independently.

03

Practice with humility

Learn through questions, patient mentorship, Git, code review, and thoughtful feedback.

04

Use tools wisely

Use AI as an assistant, verify its work, protect private information, and take responsibility for what you build.

SCRIPTURE / CRAFT / SERVICE

A WELCOMING PATH

Room to learn and belong.

We hope to welcome learners of different ages and language backgrounds; the first program's age range is still being determined. Scholarships are a future goal and are not yet available. Learners may draw on their own experiences, sharing only what they choose.

03 / A VISION FOR ATLANTA

WISDOM / JUSTICE / MODERATION

A city with roots.
A future to build.

Atlanta brings together deep history, bold ideas, and an unmistakable spirit of welcome.

Atlanta's rich Black history, educational legacy, and entrepreneurial spirit inspire this vision. The city's fashion, music, and creative communities make room for imagination, expression, and belonging.

Atlanta's motto, Resurgens—“rising again”—is a fitting emblem of renewal. We hope to widen access to practical skills, especially in communities too often overlooked, and to work alongside the people of Atlanta with humility, partnership, and respect for every person's dignity.

04 / THE FOUNDER

Isaiah Yoon

Isaiah is an engineer, builder, and coach whose life and work are shaped by faith, craft, and service.

Isaiah is a software engineer whose work spans healthcare, financial technology, and consumer software, including roles at Accompany Health, Microsoft, Marqeta, and EarnIn. A UC Berkeley graduate, he has also built tools for nonprofits and founded Yoon Basketball Training.

He is also the creator of The Amazing Word.

With roots in Los Angeles, Isaiah's faith in Jesus Christ was nurtured by his parents. His belief in salvation by grace through faith shapes his hope to use skill and creativity in service to God and neighbor.
