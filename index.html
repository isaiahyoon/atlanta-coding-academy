01 / THE CALLING

PRACTICAL SKILLS. LASTING PURPOSE.

Technology is a tool.
Service is the point.

Atlanta Coding Academy is a vision for expanding access to practical technology education and helping people use their gifts to serve God and their neighbors.

The hope is to welcome learners with dignity, including people who have been incarcerated, and help them turn their ideas into useful, beautiful work.

The vision reaches beyond the classroom: Atlanta as the greatest place in the world to raise a family.

SCRIPTURE / SPIRIT / WITNESS

Read the Word.
Walk in the Spirit.

The vision includes Bible study grounded in the complete King James Version, giving learners room to read Scripture for themselves.

John 15:26 speaks of the Holy Spirit, the Comforter and Spirit of truth, who testifies of Christ.

02 / LEARNING BY BUILDING

FUNDAMENTALS FIRST. GROWTH FOR LIFE.

A path from curiosity
to capability.

The proposed approach brings together in-person coding, Bible study, and creative projects shaped by learners' interests and the people they hope to serve.

01

Code with craft

Start with programming and web fundamentals, then explore frontend and backend development, databases, APIs, security, testing, debugging, performance, and deployment.

02

Work with people

Practice collaboration with engineers, designers, product managers, and others while developing independent judgment.

03

Practice with humility

Learn through questions, patient mentorship, Git, code review, and thoughtful feedback.

04

Use tools wisely

Use AI as an assistant, verify its work, protect private information, and take responsibility for what you build.

A WELCOMING PATH

Room to learn and belong.

The vision welcomes learners across ages and languages; the first program's age range is still to be determined. Scholarships are a future goal, not yet available. Learners can draw on their own experiences, sharing only what they choose.

03 / A VISION FOR ATLANTA

WISDOM / JUSTICE / MODERATION

A city with roots.
A future to build.

Atlanta brings together deep history, bold ideas, and an unmistakable spirit of welcome.

Atlanta's rich Black history, educational legacy, entrepreneurial energy, and creative culture inspire this vision. Its fashion, music, and communities reflect a spirit of imagination and individual expression.

Atlanta's motto, Resurgens—“rising again”—speaks to renewal. The hope is to expand access to practical skills, particularly in underserved communities, and build alongside Atlanta's people with humility, partnership, and mutual dignity.
